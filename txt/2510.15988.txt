On Bellman equation in the limit order optimization problem for high-frequency trading M.I. Balakaeva∗, & A.Yu. Veretennikov † October 21, 2025 Abstract An approximation method for construction of optimal strategies in the bid & ask limit order book in the high-frequency trading (HFT) is studied. The basis is the article [6], in which certain seemingly se- rious gaps have been found; in the present paper they are carefully corrected. However, a bit surprisingly, our corrections do not change the main answer in [6], so that, in fact, the gaps turn out to be unim- portant. An explanation of this effect is offered.
