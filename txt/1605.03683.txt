arXiv:1605.03683v3 [q-fin.TR] 31 May 2016 OPTIMALITY OF VW AP EXECUTION STRATEGIES UNDER GENERAL SHAPED MARKET IMPACT FUNCTIONS TAKASHI KATO Abstract. In this short note, we study an optimization prob- lem of expected implementation shortfall (IS) cost under general shaped market impact functions. In particular, we ﬁnd that an optimal strategy is a VW AP (volume weighted average price) ex- ecution strategy when the market model is a Black-Scholes type with stochastic clock and market trading volume is large. Let (Ω , F , (Ft)t≥0, P) be a stochastic basis. We consider (1) J(x) = sup (ζt)0≤t≤T E [ ∫ T 0 ζtStdt ] subject to dSt = St(µdVt + σd ˜BVt − g(ζt/vt)dVt), where x ≥ 0, µ < 0, σ ≥ 0, g : [0 , ∞) → [0, ∞), Vt = ∫ t 0 vrdr, (vt)t is an ( Ft)t-adapted continuous positive process with E[VT ] < ∞, ( ˜B˜t)˜t≥0 is an ( ˜F˜t)˜t≥0-Brownian motion, and ˜F˜t = FV − 1 ˜t , (ζt)t is an (Ft)t- progressively measurable non-negative process satisfying ∫ T 0 ζtdt ≤ x a.s.. Here, St (resp., ζt ) denotes a security price (resp., a trader’s selling speed of the security) at time t. g is regarded as a market impact function. Note that (1) is equivalent with minimizing expected IS cost problem (xS0 − J(x)). When vt ≡ 1 and g is quadratic, (1) is studied in Section 5.2 of [Kato14], and we see that an optimal strategy is a TW AP (time weighted average price) execution, that is, selling by constant speed, when x is not so large. This note presents a generalization of the above result. We assume the following conditions for g. (A1) g ∈ C 1((0, ∞)) ∩ C([0, ∞)), (A2) g(0) = 0 , (A3) h := g′ ≥ 0.
