Using this order type, traders can synchronize order executions across exchanges such that HFTs can no-longer engage in latency arb itrage. Put diﬀerently, the order type proposed here allows large traders to place orders such that the law of one price holds, even at “high-frequency time horizons.” Earlier proposals in the literature, aimed at reducing late ncy arbitrage, require taxes on ﬁnancial transactions, or place restrictions on the speed at which tr ades are executed, orders placed, or prices quoted.
