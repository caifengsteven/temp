# China Anti-Involution Custom Basket Index - Methodology

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the comprehensive methodology for constructing a custom basket index focused on China's anti-involution policy beneficiaries. The basket represents a strategic evolution from the historical Supply-Side Structural Reform (SSSR) 1.0 approach, emphasizing high-tech manufacturing, innovation, and market-driven consolidation.

## 1. Conceptual Framework

### 1.1 Anti-Involution Policy Context
China's anti-involution campaign, launched in July 2025, aims to:
- Combat deflationary price wars across key sectors
- Promote sustainable industry consolidation
- Shift from excessive competition to value creation
- Support innovation-driven growth

### 1.2 Investment Thesis
The basket targets companies that are:
- Direct beneficiaries of anti-involution measures
- Market leaders with pricing power and consolidation advantages
- Aligned with government innovation priorities
- Positioned for sustainable profitability improvement

## 2. Sector Allocation Methodology

### 2.1 Sector Classification
The basket covers 10 key sectors divided into three categories:

**Primary Anti-Involution Sectors (60% allocation):**
- Electric Vehicles (18%)
- Semiconductors (15%)
- Lithium/Batteries (15%)
- Solar Energy (12%)

**Traditional SSSR Sectors (25% allocation):**
- Steel (10%)
- Coal Mining (8%)
- Chemicals (7%)

**Extended Focus Areas (15% allocation):**
- Wind Energy (6%)
- Logistics (5%)
- Food Delivery (4%)

### 2.2 Allocation Scoring Model
Sector allocations are determined using a composite scoring model:

**Components (weights):**
- Policy Priority (25%): Critical/High/Moderate/Emerging classification
- Overcapacity Severity (20%): Higher overcapacity = higher priority
- Consolidation Potential (15%): Expected industry restructuring
- Innovation Intensity (15%): Technology advancement focus
- Government Support (15%): Policy backing strength
- Global Competitiveness (10%): International market position

**Formula:**
```
Sector Score = Σ(Component × Weight)
Allocation = (Sector Score / Total Scores) × 100%
```

### 2.3 Constraints
- Minimum sector allocation: 3%
- Maximum sector allocation: 25%
- Total allocation: 100%

## 3. Stock Selection Methodology

### 3.1 Universe Definition
**Eligibility Criteria:**
- Chinese companies (mainland, Hong Kong, US ADRs)
- Minimum market cap: $1B USD
- Minimum daily volume: $5M USD
- Maximum volatility: 80% annualized
- Minimum analyst coverage: 3 analysts

### 3.2 Screening Process

**Financial Health Filters:**
- Minimum revenue: $500M USD
- Maximum debt-to-equity: 300%
- Minimum profit margin: -10% (allows growth companies)
- Minimum domestic market share: 2%

**Anti-Involution Alignment Filters:**
- Minimum consolidation benefit score: 40/100
- Minimum pricing power score: 30/100
- Sector-specific innovation thresholds

### 3.3 Ranking Methodology

**Anti-Involution Alignment Score (0-100):**
```
Score = Pricing Power (25%) + Consolidation Benefit (25%) + 
        Market Position (20%) + Innovation (15%) + 
        Government Relations (15%) + Adjustments
```

**Adjustments:**
- Innovation bonus for high-tech sectors: +20%
- Ownership preference alignment: +5 points
- Market leadership bonus: +2-5 points
- Financial health adjustment: ±5 points

### 3.4 Selection Process
1. **Sector Ranking**: Rank all qualified stocks within each sector
2. **Ownership Diversity**: Ensure balanced POE/SOE representation
3. **Top Selection**: Select 2-5 top-ranked stocks per sector
4. **Final Validation**: Verify liquidity and coverage requirements

## 4. Weighting Methodology

### 4.1 Within-Sector Weighting
Stocks within each sector are weighted by:
- Market capitalization (square root): 50%
- Anti-involution alignment score: 50%

**Formula:**
```
Stock Weight = (√Market Cap × Alignment Score) / Sector Total × Sector Allocation
```

### 4.2 Weight Constraints
- Minimum individual stock weight: 1%
- Maximum individual stock weight: 8%
- Sector allocation adherence: ±2%

### 4.3 Rebalancing
- Quarterly rebalancing recommended
- Triggered rebalancing if any stock exceeds 10% weight
- Annual methodology review and updates

## 5. Risk Management

### 5.1 Diversification Controls
- Maximum 40 stocks in basket
- No single stock >8% weight
- Balanced POE/SOE representation (target: 60/40)
- Geographic diversification across exchanges

### 5.2 Liquidity Management
- Minimum $5M daily volume requirement
- Quarterly liquidity review
- Gradual position adjustments for liquidity changes

### 5.3 Policy Risk Mitigation
- Diversification across policy priorities
- Regular policy development monitoring
- Flexible methodology for policy evolution

## 7. Comparison with SSSR 1.0

### 7.1 Key Differences

| Aspect | SSSR 1.0 (2016-2018) | Anti-Involution (2025+) |
|--------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| **Focus** | Traditional heavy industries | High-tech manufacturing |
| **Approach** | Mandatory capacity cuts | Voluntary coordination |
| **Ownership** | SOE-dominated | Mixed POE/SOE |
| **Innovation** | Secondary consideration | Primary driver |
| **Timeline** | Fixed (2016-2020) | Ongoing structural shift |

### 7.2 Sector Evolution
- **High-tech shift**: +22.5% allocation to advanced sectors
- **Traditional reduction**: -44.4% from coal and steel
- **Innovation focus**: 60% allocation to innovation-intensive sectors
