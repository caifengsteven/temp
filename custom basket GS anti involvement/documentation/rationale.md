# China Anti-Involution Custom Basket Index - Investment Rationale

## Executive Summary

The China Anti-Involution Custom Basket Index represents a strategic investment opportunity targeting companies positioned to benefit from China's evolving industrial policy framework. This basket captures the structural transformation from destructive price competition to sustainable value creation, focusing on market leaders in consolidating industries with strong innovation capabilities.

## 1. Policy Context and Opportunity

### 1.1 Anti-Involution Policy Framework
China's anti-involution campaign, launched in July 2025, addresses a fundamental shift in economic priorities:

**Problem Identification:**
- Excessive competition leading to margin compression
- Deflationary price wars across key sectors
- Overcapacity in both traditional and high-tech industries
- Unsustainable business models focused on market share over profitability

**Policy Response:**
- Voluntary industry coordination to end price wars
- Government support for industry consolidation
- Investment channeling to advanced manufacturing
- Emphasis on innovation-driven growth over capacity expansion

**Investment Implication:**
Companies aligned with these policy objectives are positioned for:
- Margin recovery through pricing discipline
- Market share gains through consolidation
- Government support for strategic initiatives
- Sustainable competitive advantages

### 1.2 Historical Context: Evolution from SSSR 1.0
The anti-involution policy represents a sophisticated evolution from the Supply-Side Structural Reform (SSSR) 1.0 of 2016-2018:

**SSSR 1.0 Characteristics:**
- Focus on traditional heavy industries (coal, steel)
- Government-mandated capacity cuts
- SOE-dominated implementation
- Cyclical recovery approach

**Anti-Involution Evolution:**
- Emphasis on high-tech and service sectors
- Market-driven voluntary coordination
- Mixed POE/SOE participation
- Structural transformation focus

**Investment Advantage:**
This evolution creates opportunities in higher-growth, innovation-intensive sectors while maintaining the proven benefits of government-supported industry consolidation.

## 2. Sector-Specific Investment Themes

### 2.1 Primary Anti-Involution Sectors (60% Allocation)

**Electric Vehicles (18% allocation)**
- **Rationale**: Severe price wars initiated by industry leaders; government intervention to restore pricing discipline
- **Beneficiaries**: Market leaders like BYD, NIO, XPeng with strong brand positioning and technology advantages
- **Catalyst**: Industry agreements to end aggressive discounting; focus on profitability over market share
- **Risk/Reward**: High growth potential with government support; execution risk on voluntary compliance

**Semiconductors (15% allocation)**
- **Rationale**: Strategic national priority with government backing for technology advancement and industry consolidation
- **Beneficiaries**: Leading foundries (SMIC) and equipment manufacturers with technological capabilities
- **Catalyst**: Government investment in advanced manufacturing; import substitution drive
- **Risk/Reward**: Long-term structural growth; geopolitical and technology risks

**Lithium/Batteries (15% allocation)**
- **Rationale**: Critical EV supply chain component with overcapacity issues; CATL mine closures signal consolidation
- **Beneficiaries**: Technology leaders with integrated supply chains and cost advantages
- **Catalyst**: EV adoption acceleration; battery technology advancement; supply chain optimization
- **Risk/Reward**: High growth from EV demand; commodity price volatility

**Solar Energy (12% allocation)**
- **Rationale**: Severe global overcapacity; Chinese government supporting industry consolidation through acquisition funds
- **Beneficiaries**: Efficient manufacturers with global market access and technology leadership
- **Catalyst**: $7B polysilicon consolidation fund; capacity reduction targets
- **Risk/Reward**: Global market leadership; trade policy risks
