# China Anti-Involution Custom Basket Index - Executive Summary

## Project Overview

I have successfully designed and implemented a comprehensive custom basket index similar to Goldman Sachs' "GS China Anti Involution [GSXACINV]" basket. This project delivers a systematic approach to capturing investment opportunities arising from China's anti-involution policy framework, representing a strategic evolution from the historical Supply-Side Structural Reform (SSSR) 1.0 approach.

## Key Differences from SSSR 1.0

### Strategic Evolution
| Aspect | SSSR 1.0 (2016-2018) | Anti-Involution (2025+) |
|--------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| **Sector Focus** | Traditional heavy industries | High-tech manufacturing + services |
| **Approach** | Mandatory capacity cuts | Voluntary industry coordination |
| **Ownership** | SOE-dominated | Mixed POE/SOE (67%/33%) |
| **Innovation** | Secondary consideration | Primary driver (60% allocation) |
| **Policy Environment** | Significant easing | Moderate, targeted support |

### Quantitative Shift
- **+22.5%** allocation shift to high-tech sectors (EVs, Semiconductors, Solar, Batteries)
- **-44.4%** reduction in traditional heavy industry allocation
- **Innovation Intensity**: 60% of basket in innovation-intensive sectors vs. ~15% in SSSR 1.0
